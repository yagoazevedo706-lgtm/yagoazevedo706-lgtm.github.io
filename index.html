What it is

SBR Videos is a personal desktop tool used by the owner of a YouTube channel to upload and schedule videos to their own YouTube channel. The videos show a free multiplayer racing game made on Roblox (a fan game inspired by Steel Ball Run, with no official connection to the original work).

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the channel owner recorded, edited and approved, set to private with a scheduled publish time.
  • Reads the channel name once to confirm the login.
  • It does not access comments, subscribers, analytics, messages or any data from other users.

By using SBR Videos you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
